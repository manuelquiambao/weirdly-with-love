We think often on how culture is cultivated through generations of lived experiences and traditions. But what if we look into the future? As more of us immigrants move into Canada, as we become more globalized and interconnected, what are the lived experiences we will tell our progeny?

The inaugural theme for "weirdly, with love" focuses on lived experiences of young Black, Indigenous and other people of color.
